# Notes

Longer write-ups live here - the ones that are too big for a journal entry and that you will
actually reread. Day-to-day reflections go in the repo-root `NOTES.md`; design documents go in
`phase-09-hld-capstone/designs/`.

The briefs ask you to produce these:

| Day | File | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | `day-10-feed-estimation.md` | Sections 1-2 of the HLD template for a Twitter-scale feed |
| 31 | `day-31-lld-warmup.md` | Your LLD method, applied once |
| 40 | `day-40-lld-final.md` | The timed 45-minute LLD round, self-scored |
| 50 | `day-50-storage-tradeoffs.md` | What each storage decision buys and costs |
| 60 | `day-60-caching-tradeoffs.md` | The same, for caching |
| 70 | `day-70-messaging-tradeoffs.md` | The same, for messaging |
| 80 | `day-80-reliability-tradeoffs.md` | The same, for reliability |

The four trade-off tables are the most reusable thing the programme produces. Fill them in with
**your own measured numbers**, not the ones in the briefs - a table you populated from a benchmark
you ran is what you will actually reach for in a design discussion.

Templates are in `docs/templates/`.
