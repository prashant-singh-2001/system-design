# Phase 4 - Low-level design craft

**Days 31-40**

## Why this phase sits here

The interview asks you to design a parking lot in 45 minutes. The job asks you to design a
service in 45 minutes. It is the same skill: requirements, entities, interfaces, tests - fast,
with the concurrency thought through rather than hand-waved.

Phases 2 and 3 gave you the principles and the vocabulary. This phase is repetition under time
pressure, because that is the only thing that makes a method automatic. Day 40 is a timed round
against a rubric.

## The days

See `CURRICULUM.md` for the full day-by-day list with objectives and deliverables.

## The one idea to carry forward

**Get to the interfaces early.** Method signatures constrain the design far more than class
diagrams do, and once the signatures are right the implementation is mostly typing.
